ONEINALEAFYSEDRAGON // ABOUT & CREDITS

About & credits

oneinaleafysedragon is a free daily trivia dive. Each day has seven prompts, the same for everyone. Type one answer to each. Every listed answer counts, but the rarer it is, the deeper you sink. A perfect dive reaches the Trench at 7,000 metres.

The game is inspired by daily rarity-trivia games such as Krillion. It is an independent project with its own code, artwork, tiers and answer lists, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Krillion or its creator.

How rarity works

Rarity comes from how familiar each answer is: mainly how often it appears in written English, blended with hand-made ordering and, for cities, population. It is not based on what other players typed.
